# Hi, I'm Seok

**I build products and study battery materials.**

From a whisky journal to used-EV pricing, I turn everyday questions into apps.
In research, I use DFT calculations to understand how atomic structure shapes battery cathode materials.
I share the process on [my blog](https://seok05.github.io), including the numbers, failed runs, and decisions along the way.

## Research focus

Why do high-entropy sodium layered cathodes suppress the Jahn–Teller distortion?
My calculations so far point to the fraction of Mn³⁺ ions as a key factor.

**180 DFT cells · 1,034 MnO₆ octahedra**
